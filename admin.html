How to add bag images from Google Drive

  1. Open Google Drive and upload your bag photo
  2. Right-click the photo → Share → set to "Anyone with the link" → click Copy link
  3. Paste it in the Google Drive Link field below — a preview will appear
  4. Enter the bag name and price, then click Add to Collection
  5. The image instantly shows for all customers on the live website!
