How to upload your bag photos

  1. Create a folder called images inside your moti-bags folder on your computer
  2. Inside images, create 4 sub-folders: wedding, birthday, naming, house
  3. Copy your bag photos into the correct folder (e.g. images/wedding/bag1.jpg)
  4. In the form below — click Choose Photo, select the image, fill in the details
  5. Click Add Bag — it instantly appears on the website for customers to see!
